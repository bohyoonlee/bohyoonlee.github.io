About Me

I am an M.S. student in Statistics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison with research interests in biostatistics, causal inference, survival analysis, clinical trials, and epidemiology.

My research focuses on applying statistical methods to observational healthcare data and clinical research. I am particularly interested in causal inference methods for comparative effectiveness research, including target trial emulation.

I am interested in pursuing doctoral training in biostatistics and developing statistical methods that address important questions in medicine and public health.
